Practise MRI safety. See inside the scanner.

Two linked modules in one place. Train a pre-scan safety check with a rule-based assessor, then open up the scanner to see how the magnet, coils and physics produce an image. A handheld ESP32-S3 controller and an offline coach work in both.

Educational prototype. Safety rules are illustrative simplifications modelled on the ACR Manual on MR Safety and ASTM F2503; they do not replace local MR safety procedures, manufacturer labelling or certification. The 3D scanner is schematic and does not simulate real fields. AI replies are generated text: the rule engine's decision is always the reference.

How to use MRISafe Twin

Quick start
  1. Run python server/server.py and open localhost:8000 in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the controller chip (top right) → Connect, choose the ESP32-S3 port.
  3. Hold the controller flat → Level the controller.
  4. Pick Train or Learn. Open the assistant with the Coach chip.
